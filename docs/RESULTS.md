## 1. Alert limits and severity rule

- Warning limit: z > **3.57**; alarm limit: z > **7.42** (the same for every machine, because each machine's scores are first converted to a calibrated z).
- HIGH: at least 2 of the last 3 ten-second windows above the alarm limit. WARNING: at least 2 of the last 6 windows above the warning limit (or one window above the alarm limit). WATCH: a single window above the warning limit. The level only goes down after 5 clean windows; a HIGH that returns within 180 windows re-opens the same incident.
- Limits were calibrated on normal validation recordings only; test recordings were never used to set them.

## 5. What these results do not show

- The models detect how unlike its learned normal sound a machine is. They do not diagnose the physical fault, predict failure time or give a probability of failure.
- All results come from the MIMII dataset (laboratory recordings of 8 machines). Performance on other machines, microphones or factories has not been measured.
- Simulated monitoring replays recorded MIMII clips; it is not a live microphone connection.
